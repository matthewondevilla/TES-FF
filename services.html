Production
🎙️
Audio & Visual Support

Our AV team delivers professional sound reinforcement, projection, and display solutions calibrated to each unique space on campus.

  • Microphone arrays (wired, wireless, lavalier)
  • Digital mixing consoles (32–96 channel)
  • 4K laser projection & LED video wall
  • Confidence monitors & speaker timers
  • Live-stream encoding & webcast setup
  • Hearing-loop (ADA) systems available

  • UCPD liaison & officer request processing

Our operations crew handles all physical event logistics — furniture, staging, signage, and teardown — so you can arrive to a ready venue.

  • Staging & riser assembly / strike
  • Pipe & drape / backdrop installation

  • Wayfinding & event signage placement
  • Post-event venue restoration & cleanup

Eligibility

Who We Serve

Student Organizations

Registered student organizations (RSOs) receive priority booking windows and reduced facility-use rates. Must be in good standing with the Student Organizations, Leadership & Engagement office.

Faculty & Staff

Academic departments and administrative units may book venues directly. A campus chartstring is required for internal billing. Department events receive institutional rate pricing.

Campus Departments

Centers, institutes, research units, and organized research units (ORUs) are welcome. Includes entities under the UC Regents umbrella operating on the Westwood campus.

Approved External Partners

Non-UCLA organizations with a pre-approved campus partnership agreement may access select venues at external rates. Contact our office to begin the partner approval process.
