Before you submit

Standard requests require at least 10 business days. Signature venues such as Royce Hall and Pauley Pavilion require 30 to 45 days.

Lead time requirements

Large or complex events may require extra review for space availability, staffing, security, and production needs.

Insurance required

Events with external guests may require a Certificate of Insurance naming The Regents of the University of California as additional insured.

Fee structure

Student organizations may receive reduced rates. Departments are billed through chartstring. External partners may pay standard event rates.

Cancellation policy

Cancellations with 14 or more days notice may receive a full refund. Short notice cancellations may be partially refundable or nonrefundable.
