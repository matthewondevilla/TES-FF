Who we support

A campus operations desk built for academic life.

Whether you are planning a student showcase, a faculty symposium, a department reception, or an approved community program, the portal helps route your request to the right venue, production, and safety teams.

Student organizations

Space advising, room layouts, basic AV, and student event readiness.

Faculty and staff

Lecture, conference, and reception planning for academic programs.

Campus departments

Production support for ceremonies, trainings, and signature events.

Approved partners

Guided intake for external programs aligned with campus policy.

How it works

From idea to event-day execution.

Use one request to start the conversation. Our review flow considers space fit, technical scope, staffing, accessibility, risk, and campus calendar impacts.

Start a Booking Request
  1. Submit the request

    Share event goals, preferred venue, date, guest count, and production needs.

  2. Receive routing guidance

    The request is reviewed for availability, policy requirements, and support teams.

  3. Confirm scope and estimate

    Finalize layouts, staffing, technical equipment, and event-day access details.

  4. Operate with confidence

    Event staff coordinate setup, support, safety checkpoints, and closeout.
