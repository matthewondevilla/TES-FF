Policy Document Information

These policies were last updated March 1, 2025 and supersede all prior versions. For questions or clarifications, contact eventservices@ucla.edu or call (310) 825-1234. Policies are subject to change in accordance with UC Regents directives.

Section 1

General Event Guidelines

All events hosted on UCLA property — whether indoors or outdoors — must comply with the following baseline requirements regardless of size, scope, or hosting organization.

Advance Booking Requirements

All event requests must be submitted with sufficient lead time for coordinator review and approval:

  • Standard venues (conference rooms, seminar halls): Minimum 10 business days in advance.
  • Mid-scale venues (Ackerman Ballroom, Carnesale Commons): Minimum 15 business days.
  • Signature venues (Royce Hall, Pauley Pavilion): Minimum 30–45 business days. Academic-year priority windows apply.
  • Outdoor spaces with amplified sound: Minimum 15 business days; noise ordinance review required.
Eligible Event Types

The following event types are eligible for campus venue reservations:

  • Academic conferences, lectures, symposia, and seminars
  • Student organization programs, meetings, cultural celebrations, and fundraisers
  • Departmental retreats, town halls, and community gatherings
  • Performing arts productions, film screenings, and concerts
  • University ceremonies (commencements, inaugurations, award events)
  • Approved external partner events with confirmed campus affiliation
Prohibited Activities

The following activities are not permitted at any UCLA campus event:

  • Commercial sales or solicitation without prior authorization from Student Affairs
  • Distribution of materials not approved under the campus posting policy
  • Events that violate the University of California's non-discrimination policy
  • Use of open flames, pyrotechnics, or fog machines without Fire Marshal approval
  • Amplified sound outdoors after 10:00 PM (Sunday–Thursday) or 11:00 PM (Friday–Saturday)
  • Events that interfere with academic instruction or scheduled university functions
Attendance & Capacity

Guest counts may not exceed the legally posted occupancy of any venue. The event host is responsible for accurate attendance estimates. Events projecting attendance within 10% of venue capacity must submit a crowd management plan and may be required to engage additional security personnel at the organizer's expense.

Section 2

Insurance & Liability

Certificate of Insurance Required

All events that include external (non-UCLA) guests must provide a valid Certificate of Insurance prior to receiving a confirmed venue reservation. Certificates must be received no later than 5 business days before the event date.

Minimum Coverage Requirements
Event Category General Liability Minimum Additional Insured
Small internal meetings (<50 attendees, UCLA-only) Waived for registered dept./RSOs —
Mid-scale events (50–499 guests) $1,000,000 per occurrence The Regents of UC
Large events (500–4,999 guests) $2,000,000 per occurrence The Regents of UC
Major events (5,000+ guests / Pavilion scale) $5,000,000 aggregate The Regents of UC + UCPD
Events with alcohol service $2,000,000 + liquor liability rider The Regents of UC
Student Organization Waiver

Registered Student Organizations (RSOs) in good standing that host events exclusively for enrolled UCLA students may apply for an insurance waiver through the Student Organizations, Leadership & Engagement (SOLE) office. Contact SOLE at least 15 business days before the event to initiate the waiver process. Approval is not guaranteed.

Damage Liability

Event organizers are financially responsible for any damage to UCLA property, equipment, or facilities caused during setup, the event, or breakdown. A damage inspection is conducted within 48 hours post-event. Damage charges are billed to the hosting organization's chartstring or invoiced directly to the responsible party.

Event Modifications

Changes to confirmed event details (date, time, venue, guest count, or services) must be submitted in writing to your assigned coordinator. Modifications requested fewer than 5 business days before the event are subject to a $75 administrative change fee per modification and cannot be guaranteed.

Weather & Force Majeure

Outdoor events canceled due to inclement weather confirmed by the National Weather Service (wind speeds exceeding 35 mph, lightning within 10 miles, or heavy rain) are eligible for a one-time venue credit valid for 12 months, subject to availability. Force majeure events (natural disasters, public health emergencies, University closure) will be resolved on a case-by-case basis per UC Regents policy.

Section 4

Safety & Security

UCPD Officer Requirement

Events with 500 or more attendees, events open to the general public, or events featuring cash transactions require a minimum of one UCPD officer on-site for the duration of the event. Additional officers may be required based on event risk assessment. UCPD officer costs are borne by the event organizer at the current overtime rate.

Safety Plan Requirements

A written Event Safety Plan is required for all events exceeding 200 guests or involving any of the following elements:

Open Flames or Pyrotechnics
Fire Marshal inspection required 10 days prior.
Amplified Sound >85 dB
Sound level agreement required for outdoor events.
Elevated Structures / Rigging
Engineer certification required for temporary structures.
Water Features / Inflatables
Risk management pre-approval required.
Medical & Emergency Preparedness

Events with 1,000+ attendees must have a designated first aid station staffed by a minimum of one certified first aid/CPR responder. UCLA Environmental Health & Safety (EH&S) can advise on medical standby requirements. Emergency Action Plans must be shared with the on-site Event Services coordinator at least 48 hours before the event.

ADA Accessibility

All campus events must comply with the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA) and UC accessibility standards. Event organizers must ensure: accessible seating is available and clearly marked; accessible pathways are free of obstruction; assistive listening systems are available upon request; and signage meets WCAG contrast guidelines. Failure to meet accessibility requirements may result in event cancellation.

Section 5

Catering & Alcohol Policy

Approved Catering Vendors

UCLA Catering is the preferred and first-right-of-refusal vendor for all on-campus food service events. Event organizers wishing to use an external caterer must first obtain a waiver from UCLA Catering, then submit the external vendor's health permit, insurance certificate, and proposed menu to Event Services for approval at least 10 business days before the event.

Alcohol Service Requirements

All events serving alcohol must comply with the following requirements without exception:

  • Written approval from the Dean of Students Office or department Dean (as applicable).
  • A licensed and bonded bartending service — alcohol service by event staff or volunteers is prohibited.
  • Liquor liability insurance rider (minimum $2M) added to the event's Certificate of Insurance.
  • Alcohol service must end a minimum of 30 minutes before the event concludes.
  • Non-alcoholic beverages and food must be available throughout the entire service period.
  • At least one uniformed UCPD officer must be present when alcohol is served at public-facing events.
  • No alcohol service to persons under 21. Valid ID must be checked at point of service.
Sustainability Requirements

In alignment with UCLA's Sustainable Events Policy, all catered events of 100 or more guests must utilize reusable or compostable serviceware. Single-use plastics are prohibited in all campus event spaces. Event organizers must arrange for proper waste sorting (landfill, recycling, and compost) through UCLA Facilities Management or a contracted waste services vendor.

Yes, with restrictions. Amplified sound at outdoor events must comply with the City of Los Angeles Noise Ordinance and UCLA's campus sound policy:
  • Sound levels may not exceed 85 dB at the perimeter of the event zone.

Events are expected to conclude — including teardown — by the contracted end time. Overtime is billed at 1.5× the standard hourly venue rate in 30-minute increments, applied automatically once 15 minutes past the scheduled end time has elapsed. If a subsequent event is booked in the same space, early access to overtime is not guaranteed and your event may be required to vacate immediately.

External organizations may access UCLA event facilities only if they have an approved campus partnership agreement on file with UCLA Event Services. Initiating this process requires submitting an External Partner Application at least 30 days before the desired event date. External partner events are subject to full commercial venue rates, mandatory insurance requirements, and must align with the University's mission and values. Cold inquiries without an established agreement are not guaranteed consideration.
